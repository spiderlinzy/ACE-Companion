# About the Author

**ZhiXuan ZHU** works at the intersection of AI engineering, security and governance, focusing on large language model (LLM) risk management and the controls needed to preserve trustworthiness across the AI system lifecycle; research interests include algorithmic fairness and the inclusive deployment of AI in high-risk settings.

She is the author of *AI Compliance Engineering: From Regulation to Evidence — A Methodology for Trustworthy and Ethical AI Systems*, examining large-model risk and lifecycle controls from both technical and legal dimensions. She participates in the IEEE P3410.1 working group, following the development of standards for large-scale financial risk-management models, with a focus on data and model governance, risk assessment, algorithmic bias and model evaluation; her research on inclusive AI deployment and decision fairness has been accepted at an IEEE international conference. She has completed certification training on the EU AI Act, OWASP LLM security and AI ethics, with courses from the University of Oxford, the University of Helsinki and an ASEAN program.

In engineering practice, she has built financial-AI agent prototypes for due-diligence and risk-control scenarios, with built-in traceable audit logs and risk-interception modules — the evidence-based controls proposed in this book grow directly out of such front-line practice. She takes part in women-in-technology communities including the IEEE Women in Engineering group, the Women in Science and Technology Committee of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), and Google Women Techmakers, focusing on diversity in AI research and practice.
